Sheet A-02 — Studio Projects

Studio Work

Three studio projects, from a first study model through to a complete studio design.

01

First Project

Mixed-media study model

My first architectural model project, translating a reference image into a physical three-dimensional form. The exercise centred on form, proportion, and spatial relationship, working through different materials and construction techniques — and it's where I picked up my basic model-making skills.

02

Battle of Grids

Geometric pattern study

My second project, carrying a set of geometric patterns from flat line studies through to three-dimensional relief forms. It's where I developed my creativity and model-making skills, and built a stronger grasp of basic design principles.

03

The Linking Studio

Studio design — drawing set & model

A design project built around the idea of connection and flow. I explored how bridges and pathways can link different spaces within a studio, creating a smoother movement experience for its users while strengthening the relationship between the building's functions.

Creative Process
